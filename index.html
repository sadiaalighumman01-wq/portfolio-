About Sadia Ihsan

Shaping the Future Through Educational Excellence & Mentorship

I am a committed educationist with an M.Phil in Education from the University of Lahore and a Master’s in Education from the University of Education. With experience teaching in esteemed institutions like The Scott Science College and Great Nation Model School, I specialize in English instruction and Arts curriculum design.

My core mission as an educator is to beat the hurdle in education and improve the confidence level of students. I believe that every learner has innate potential waiting to be ignited when given the right psychological safety, peer motivation, and interactive teaching methodologies.

Along with my formal university education, I have completed Hifz-ul-Quran from Jamia Fatimia Masjid Garbi Gujranwala, instilling within me deep discipline, ethical values, dedication, and patient mentorship skills that guide my daily teaching.

"The Effect Of Peer Socialization And Colleague Motivation On Student Performance At Elementary Level"
